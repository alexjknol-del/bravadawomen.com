Founder & Lead Voice, Bravada Women

Zora

“Pleasure isn’t a bonus feature of your life. It’s part of your health.”

Zora started Bravada Women after years of feeling like sexual wellness content came in exactly two flavors: clinical and clinical-shy, or loud and cringeworthy. She wanted something that talked to women the way a sharp, funny, well-informed friend would.

She writes about pleasure, confidence, and intimacy the way she'd want a friend to explain it to her: no shame, no fluff, and no pretending an orgasm is a personality trait. Just useful information, delivered with a wink.

Bravada Women isn't a shop. It's a place to get informed and get comfortable, before you go get whatever it is you're after.

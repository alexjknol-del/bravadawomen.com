Your Intimate Skincare Routine, in Five Simple Rules

If your intimate care routine is a leftover bar of soap, you're not alone — and you're also not doing your body any favors. The vulva has its own self-cleaning system and a delicate pH balance that most soaps, especially fragranced ones, throw off.

Rule one: wash externally only, with a mild, fragrance-free, pH-balanced cleanser. The inside doesn't need cleaning — it does that on its own, and disrupting it tends to cause more irritation and infection risk than it solves.

Rule two: skip douching entirely. It's one of the most persistent myths in intimate care, and it consistently correlates with higher rates of infection rather than "freshness."

Rule three: breathable fabric, especially for underwear worn for long stretches or during exercise. Cotton allows moisture to escape; synthetic fabrics trap it, which raises irritation risk.

Rule four and five: be selective about what touches this area during sex and throughout the day — lubricants, wipes, and any product marketed with strong fragrance deserve a second look at the ingredient list.

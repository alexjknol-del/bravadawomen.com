Daily Intimate Care, Explained

Most women have a skincare routine dialed in, but intimate care tends to get an afterthought: whatever soap happens to be in the shower. That's a shame, because the skin in that area is more delicate, has a different pH balance, and reacts badly to a lot of what's marketed as "feminine hygiene."

The single biggest rule: less is more. The vulva is self-cleaning on the inside, and harsh soaps, fragranced washes, and douching all disrupt a delicate microbiome that's doing a good job on its own. A gentle, pH-balanced wash for the external area is usually all that's needed.

Fabric matters more than people expect. Breathable, natural fabrics like cotton reduce moisture buildup, which lowers the risk of irritation and infection compared to synthetic materials worn for long stretches, especially during exercise or in warm weather.

Daily intimate care isn't just about washing — it's also about what touches that area throughout the day: the products you use during sex, the lubricant you reach for, the wipes in your bag. Treating this as a legitimate part of your self-care routine, rather than a topic to rush past, tends to pay off in fewer irritations and more comfort day to day.
